# Fusion Tuition

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product purpose

The public Singapore tuition website helps visitors find supported curricula and subjects and contact Fusion Tuition for a free trial class.

## Verified offering

Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics tuition supports IGCSE, GCE O Level, A Level, and IB. The existing classes page promises groups of no more than three students, flexible scheduling, and free consultations outside lesson hours. `src/lib/agent-ready.ts` owns the public curriculum catalogue and contact details.

## Constraints

Preserve the Fusion Tuition logo, shared orange brand, course names, syllabus codes, and contact destinations. Do not invent prices, availability, results, testimonials, or student photographs. This redesign is limited to `/classes`; retain shared navigation, footer, discovery metadata, and other routes. Contact remains WhatsApp, telephone, or email; no new booking backend.

## Audience assumptions

The classes redesign assumes visitors include students and parents comparing curricula, often on a narrow screen. This is a design assumption, not a newly confirmed business claim.
