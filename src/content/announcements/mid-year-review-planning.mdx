Mid-year is usually the most useful checkpoint for planning the second half of the academic year.

For many students, this is the right time to review:

- which topics still feel fragile
- whether revision habits are consistent enough
- which subject needs the most support first
- whether the current study schedule is realistic

If you want a clearer sense of available subjects first, take a look at [classes](/classes).
